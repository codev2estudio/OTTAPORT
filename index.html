How we work

From spark to screen.

  1. 01

    Idea

    Every project begins as an idea — a character, a world, a feeling worth exploring. We give ideas room to grow before anything else happens.

  2. 02

    Story

    Ideas become stories. We shape structure, character and emotion, asking one question over and over: is this worth telling?

  3. 03

    Animation

    Stories come alive through animation — design, motion and sound working together until the work has a pulse of its own.

  4. 04

    Experience

    A finished piece is only the beginning. We build with the ambition that worlds can live on — across screens, platforms and audiences.

About Ottaport

Young company.
Serious vision.

Ottaport is an independent animation and entertainment company — young in age, but deliberate in direction. It was founded on a simple conviction: original worlds deserve to be built properly, and original stories deserve a home.

We are at the beginning, and we say that plainly. Ottaport is not a large studio with decades of history — it is a focused creative company taking its first real steps in front of the world, learning and improving with every piece of work it releases.

What we lack in history, we make up for in intent. Everything we publish is original, everything we build is ours, and every step of this journey is aimed at something much larger than any single project — worlds that people can return to, again and again.
